# User Guide

## How to Practice Notes

1. **Select a note**: Click on any note displayed on the staff notation. The selected note will be highlighted in brass color.

2. **View fingering**: The fingering diagram shows which saxophone keys to press. Keys are labeled L1-L4 (left hand) and R1-R4 (right hand). Pressed keys appear in brass color; unpressed keys appear dark.

3. **Hear the pitch**: Click the "Play {note}" button or click the note on the staff to hear a reference tone through your speakers/headphones.

4. **Play your saxophone**: Once you know the fingering, play the note on your instrument.

5. **Check tuning**: Observe the tuner gauge to see if you're in tune. The center line indicates perfect pitch; left shows flat (too low), right shows sharp (too high).

## Using the Tuner Feature

The tuner helps you develop accurate pitch control:

- **Tuner Gauge**: A horizontal bar shows pitch deviation in cents. Your goal is to keep the indicator centered.
- **Cents Value**: Displays the exact deviation from the target pitch.
- **Tuning History Chart**: Tracks your tuning accuracy over time, helping you identify patterns and improvements.

**Tip**: Watch the history chart to see if you consistently play sharp or flat, then adjust accordingly.

## Understanding the Fingering Diagram

The fingering diagram displays a simplified saxophone key layout:

| Key | Hand | Position |
|-----|------|----------|
| L1-L4 | Left hand | Top row |
| R1-R4 | Right hand | Bottom row |

The diagram shows fingerings for notes C4 through C5 (one octave). When you select a note on the staff, the corresponding keys will light up in brass color to indicate which should be pressed.

## Feature Walkthrough

### Staff Notation Panel
- Displays a treble clef with 8 notes (C4 to C5)
- Click any note to select it and hear its pitch
- Selected notes are highlighted

### Fingering Diagram Panel
- Visual representation of saxophone keys
- Shows which keys to press for the selected note
- Updates automatically when you change notes or instruments

### Audio Player Panel
- Plays reference pitches using Web Audio API
- Button labeled with current note ("Play C4", "Play D4", etc.)
- Useful for pitch matching before practicing

### Tuner Panel
- Gauge shows real-time pitch deviation
- History chart displays tuning trend over time
- Values range from -50 (flat) to +50 (sharp) cents

### Instrument Selector
- Toggle between alto and tenor saxophone
- Button group with two options: "alto" and "tenor"
- Currently shows the same fingering data; future updates may include instrument-specific alternate fingerings
